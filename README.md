### CHEM-000511: Finite-temperature ideal glass transition in molecular liquids
- **Subfield:** Liquid-state and interfacial physical chemistry
- **Open:** Establish whether realistic three-dimensional molecular liquids possess an equilibrium ideal-glass transition with vanishing configurational entropy, or determine what avoids it.
- **Status:** The entropy-crisis question dates to Kauzmann’s 1948 analysis. Enhanced sampling has extended equilibrium entropy measurements, but extrapolation to a genuine singularity remains disputed.
- **Type:** theory | experimental
- **Why:** It would distinguish major explanations of glass formation.
- **Key works:** L. Berthier et al. (2017), *Configurational entropy measurements in extremely supercooled liquids that break the glass ceiling*, [arXiv:1704.08257](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.08257).
- **Related:** Statistical mechanics: random first-order transitions and metastable-state counting.
- **Aliases:** Kauzmann paradox; entropy crisis.
